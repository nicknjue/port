About STP

Empowering Students Beyond The Classroom

The Student Transition Program (STP) is designed to support students throughout their university journey. Through engagement activities, career readiness initiatives, entrepreneurship development and alumni connections, STP helps students discover opportunities, build confidence and prepare for life beyond campus.

Our Mission

To create meaningful experiences that help students transition successfully from admission to graduation and beyond.
